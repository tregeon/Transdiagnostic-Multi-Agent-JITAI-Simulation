# Transdiagnostic-Multi-Agent-JITAI-Simulation
This repository contains supplementary material of the simulation study. 

The overarching intervention design was inspired by **Zarski et al. (2025)**, with several adaptations as part of the present simulation setup regarding the agents, intervention frequency, and structuring of the modules. 

- Zarski, A.-C., Sextl-Plötz, T., Schmidt-Hantke, J., Hess, N. S., & Buntrock, C. (2025). Participatory development and proof-
of-concept of a dyadic-based, AI-driven, just-in-time adaptive intervention mechanism for preventing anxiety and
depressive disorders via app: Study protocol for a feasibility study. Frontiers in Public Health, 13, 1626428. 
<https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1626428>

The used personas to generate synthetic LLM-based patient interactions were adopted from **"TalkDep" (Wang et al., 2025)** and then further refined. 

- Wang, X., Perez, A., Parapar, J., & Crestani, F. (2025). TalkDep: Clinically Grounded LLM Personas for Conversation-Centric
Depression Screening. Proceedings of the 34th ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge
Management, 6554–6558. <https://doi.org/10.1145/3746252.3761617>

- GitHub: <https://github.com/Anxo06/TalkDep/>
### Patient Agent:
The LLM-driven patient agents used in the longitudinal simulation study were powered by Meta Llama 3.3 70B Instruct, the same model evaluated for intervention personalization.
