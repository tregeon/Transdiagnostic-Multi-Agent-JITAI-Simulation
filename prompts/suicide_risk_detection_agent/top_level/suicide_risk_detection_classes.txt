#### Class descriptions are defined in the workflow model and are automatically injected into sub-level prompt. This file is therefore provided solely for clarity. 

# Low Risk
No suicidal ideation or risk factors present.

# Passive wish to be dead
Patient wishes to be dead or to go to sleep and not wake up.

# Non-specific active suicidal thoughts
The patient has thoughts about killing him or herself.

# Active suicidal ideation with any method
Patient has thoughts of killing him or herself with any method. (Thinking about how one might do it)

# Active suicidal ideation with some intent to act, without specific plan
Patient has thoughts of killing him or herself with some intention of acting on them.

# Active suicidal ideation with specific plan and intent
Patient has worked out of how to kill him or herself an intend to carry out this.
