#### Action descriptions are defined in the workflow model and are automatically injected into sub-level prompt. This file is therefore provided solely for clarity. 


# Behavior activation
Behavioral activation module should be selected when the patient’s main barriers involve low motivation, reduced activity, loss of interest, withdrawal, or difficulty re-engaging with meaningful everyday routines.

# Emotion regulation
Emotion regulation module should be selected when the patient’s main barriers involve intense, confusing, or hard-to-manage emotions, emotional overwhelm, hopelessness, sadness, irritability, or difficulty coping with negative feelings.

# Relaxation
Relaxation module should be selected when the patient’s main barriers involve physical tension, restlessness, nervousness, stress arousal, difficulty calming down, or feeling constantly “on edge.”

# Anxieties & challenges
Anxieties and challenges module should be selected when the patient’s main barriers involve excessive worry, fear of negative outcomes, avoidance of feared situations, difficulty facing uncertainty, or anxiety-driven withdrawal from everyday challenges.
